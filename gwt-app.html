Account Deletion Instructions

How to permanently delete your GWT account

Hello,

You've requested information on how to delete your Growing Wealth Together (GWT) account. Please follow the steps below carefully to permanently remove your account and all associated data from our systems.

1
Log Into Your Account
Open the Growing Wealth Together mobile app and sign in using your email and PIN.
2
Navigate to Profile
Tap the profile icon at the top of your dashboard, then open Settings from the menu.
3
Find Account Management
Scroll to the Account Management section where you'll see the Delete Account option.
4
Initiate Deletion
Tap Delete Account. You'll be asked to enter your PIN and tick the confirmation box.
5
Confirm Deletion
Read the warning carefully, then confirm by tapping Delete Account in the confirmation dialog. You will be logged out immediately.
⚠️
Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your profile, photo, and all account activity will be permanently removed from GWT.
📊
Data that will be deleted:
  • Your personal profile information and photo
  • All service bookings, job applications, and event registrations
  • Reward points, membership history, and referral records
  • Any connected services or integrations

If you're experiencing issues or have questions before proceeding, our support team is here to help.

Need assistance? Visit gwtogether.org or contact our support team.
